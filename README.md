# Nostalgic Hub

Nostalgic Hub is a cultural discovery and revival platform for Northeast India. It helps people rediscover forgotten traditional games, folk crafts, heritage foods, childhood memories, and local artisan culture through a digital experience that feels human, nostalgic, and community-driven.

## What the project is about

The project is built around a simple idea: many rich traditions, crafts, games, stories, and local skills are fading away because they are not visible, not documented, or not connected to modern digital life.

Nostalgic Hub brings these traditions back into the public conversation by creating a digital home for:

- traditional games and play culture
- bamboo, pottery, and indigenous crafts
- local food stories and recipes
- cultural knowledge and memory preservation
- artisan discovery and marketplace access
- community participation and playful learning

## What it does

The platform combines discovery, storytelling, creativity, and commerce in one experience.

### Core features
- Explore forgotten games and childhood traditions
- Browse craft stories and cultural artifacts
- Discover regional food memories and heritage recipes
- Create or remix cultural ideas with AI assistance
- Save, track, and revisit favorite cultural memories
- Find local artisans and heritage creators
- Browse a curated marketplace for regional products
- Support community challenges, engagement, and retention
- Encourage trust through admin verification and cultural moderation

## Why it exists

This project is for both preservation and opportunity:

- It helps preserve Northeast Indian culture in a modern digital form.
- It gives artisans and local creators a digital presence.
- It reconnects younger generations with their roots.
- It turns cultural heritage into a discoverable, engaging experience instead of a static archive.

## Who it is for

### Primary users
- Families and communities looking to reconnect with traditions
- Millennials and younger users interested in heritage and nostalgia
- Cultural enthusiasts and local learners
- Artisans and small craft businesses

### Secondary users
- Tourists and cultural travelers
- Students and researchers
- Community organizers
- NGOs and institutions supporting regional heritage

## Business and impact angle

Beyond being a cultural platform, Nostalgic Hub also supports a marketplace model where local artisans can showcase and sell authentic regional products. This creates both cultural value and economic opportunity for communities that are often excluded from mainstream digital commerce.

## Technical direction

This project is implemented as a full-stack web application using Next.js and is designed to be:

- mobile-friendly
- community-driven
- multilingual-friendly
- scalable beyond a single region
- suitable for a hackathon MVP with real functionality rather than a static landing page

## Project goal

The goal is to create a polished MVP that feels like a living cultural museum and community marketplace — one that helps people discover what was forgotten, celebrate what still exists, and build a future where culture remains alive and commercially accessible.

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.
