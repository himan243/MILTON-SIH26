WHAT MAKES NOSTALGIC HUB DIFFERENT?
INDEX	EXISTING PROBLEM	NOSTALGIC HUB SOLUTION
DISCOVERY	Cultural products are fragmented	One discovery platform
ACCESS	Artisans lack digital reach	Direct artisan marketplace
TRUST	Cultural information can be inaccurate	Verification + admin approval
NOSTALGIA	Memories aren't commercially connected	Games + builds + stories
AI	AI platforms are generic	AI connected to cultural creativity
COMMUNITY	Traditional games are disappearing	Find people + game sessions
ARTISANS	Middlemen reduce visibility	Direct vendor/customer connection
EDUCATION	Cultural knowledge is scattered	Structured stories/tutorials
RETENTION	Marketplaces rely mainly on purchases	XP + badges + challenges + collections
LOCAL → GLOBAL	Regional products struggle to scale	Northeast → India → global architecture

The product specification itself positions the platform as a combination of cultural preservation + nostalgia + AI creativity + traditional games + local community + artisan marketplace, rather than just another marketplace.
